Sonder / privacy

Privacy Policy

Your writing belongs to you. This explains how Sonder handles and protects data.

Last updated: October 21, 2026

What we do not collect

Sonder requires no account and includes no ads, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. Generative AI does not read or analyze your journal. The developer does not receive your journal text or use it to build a profile about you.

Data on this device

Journal text, moods, favorites, hidden passages, recycle bin items, trial-day records, and app settings stay on this device by default. Sonder operates no account system or proprietary sync server.

Optional iCloud Sync

Only when you enable iCloud does journal data use Apple CloudKit to store in your personal iCloud and sync across devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Apple provides those cloud services under its own terms and privacy policy.

Device permissions

App Lock uses Face ID, Touch ID, or the device passcode through the system. Sonder receives only whether authentication succeeded and never receives biometric data. Photo access is requested only when you choose to save a card to your library.

Exports and backups

Markdown is a readable export that excludes hidden passages and recycle bin items. JSON is a complete restore backup containing visible writing and hidden passages, while excluding recycle bin items and system settings. Once a file leaves Sonder, it is handled by the app or location you choose, so share it only with places you trust.

Purchases

The Apple App Store processes the Full Version purchase. Sonder verifies the transaction and entitlement but never receives your card or other payment details.

Deletion and retention

Deleting a whole journal day moves it to the recycle bin for 30×24 hours, during which you can restore or permanently delete it; it is permanently deleted after that period. An individually deleted passage is removed immediately. Hidden passages remain part of your journal and are included in a complete JSON backup.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially, this page and its date will be updated. Privacy questions can be sent through the official website or support channel below.
