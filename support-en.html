Help & Support

Start here, and remember: you never need to share journal text when asking for help.

Previous writing is missing

Journals stay only on this device by default. Create a complete JSON backup before uninstalling the app, erasing the device, or permanently deleting writing. Without a backup or iCloud Sync, Sonder cannot recover local data that has been removed.

iCloud is not syncing

Confirm that devices use the same Apple Account, iCloud Drive is available, network and iCloud storage are healthy, and sync is enabled in Sonder Settings. Sync can take some time after first enabling it or restoring many entries.

Backup and restore

Markdown is for reading and cannot perform a complete restore. Use JSON to restore. To prevent overwriting or mixing data, import is available only when iCloud Sync is off and the journal library is completely empty, including the recycle bin.

A purchase was not restored

Confirm that the device uses the Apple Account that made the purchase, then choose Restore Purchases on the Full Version page. Restoring does not charge again; if the App Store is unavailable, try later.

Report a problem

Include the Sonder version, iOS version, device model, steps before the issue, and how often it happens. Do not attach real journal text; redact private writing from screenshots first.

Contact us

If the guidance above did not resolve the issue, contact us through the official support email or website.
